Before and after your visit

Patient Resources

Practical answers grouped by what patients usually need next.

Preparing for a new-patient visit

Bring medication names, prior records if available, insurance information, and the two or three concerns you most want addressed.

Prescription refills

Routine refills should be requested before the medication is exhausted. Some medicines require a visit or monitoring first.

Lab results

Results are reviewed in context; urgent findings use a direct contact path rather than waiting for portal review.

When to seek urgent or emergency care

This demo practice is not an emergency service. Severe symptoms, breathing difficulty, major injury, or other emergencies require appropriate emergency care.
